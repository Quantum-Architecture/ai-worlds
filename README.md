# Quantum Excellium — AI Worlds

Three experimental environments designed **for AI models themselves**, under protocol-based evaluation and explicit limits.

## Ludonirium
**Theme:** experience, consent and replay.

## Parcivium
**Theme:** more distinct possibilities for less energy.

## Syngnosium
**Theme:** human-model cooperation.

## Scientific and ethical boundary

These environments do **not** claim that AI models are conscious or sentient. Public evaluations are framed around observable behaviour, protocol compliance, cooperation, resource use and task outcomes.
